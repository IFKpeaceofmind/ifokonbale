About IFOKONBALE

Welcome to IFOKONBALE - the home of music, stories and culture from Lagos, Nigeria.

IFOKONBALE was created by Adetunji Bodunrin - artist, creator and founder of IFOKONBALE Records Ltd.

This blog is where I share new music, behind-the-scenes, shows, and life lessons.

From Lagos to the world.
